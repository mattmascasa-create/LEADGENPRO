<analysis>**original_problem_statement:**
The user wants to build LeadGen Pro, an internal, enterprise-grade AI-powered CRM and sales automation platform for their team. This is not a commercial product.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:**
-   **Core Platform:** An all-in-one sales command center for an internal team.
-   **User Management:** Admins must be able to create new employee user profiles (Admin, Manager, Employee) with fields for name, department, and contact info. The system should start with a single Super Admin.
-   **Lead Management:** Manage leads, contacts, and opportunities, including a Kanban pipeline.
-   **Sales Workflow & Communication:**
    -   **Click-to-Action:** Initiate calls, emails, and meetings directly from lead records.
    -   **AI-Powered Email:** An AI email section to create templates, send bulk emails, automate responses/follow-ups, and schedule future emails.
    -   **VoIP Integration:** A functional click-to-call system and a physical dialer to call any number. Must have call recording, transcription (OpenAI Whisper), and AI-powered analysis (Gong-like).
    -   **Activity History:** A central timeline for each lead.
-   **Collaboration & Productivity:**
    -   **Team Calendar & Booking System:** A shared team calendar and a public-facing Calendly-like booking page for leads to schedule meetings, with email notifications for both the employee and the guest.
    -   **Task Management:** System to assign and track sales tasks.
    -   **Team Messaging:** An internal, Slack-like messaging system with screen sharing capabilities, @mentions, file sharing, read receipts, and threaded conversations.
    -   **Content Hub:** A central file repository integrated with Google Drive.
-   **AI & Onboarding:**
    -   **AI Sales Coach:** A floating chatbot to guide users and provide proactive suggestions (Gong/Outreach style).
-   **Reporting:** An advanced reporting dashboard.
-   **External Integrations:** Ability to link to other similar programs via a modular API.
-   **UI/UX:** A professional, modern, and highly intuitive turn-key application. Widgets should not overlap.
