<analysis>**original_problem_statement:**
The user wants to build LeadGen Pro, an internal, enterprise-grade AI-powered CRM and sales automation platform for their team. This is not a commercial product.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:**
-   **Core Platform:** An all-in-one sales command center for an internal team.
-   **User Management:** Admins must be able to create new employee user profiles.
-   **Lead Management:** Manage leads, contacts, and opportunities. Includes bulk import/export, manual entry, lead distribution by admins, and a drag-and-drop Kanban pipeline.
-   **Sales Workflow & Communication:**
    -   **Click-to-Action:** Initiate calls, emails, and meetings directly from lead records.
    -   **Bulk Email:** Select multiple leads and send mass emails using a simple composer.
    -   **VoIP Integration:** A fully functional click-to-call system with call recording, transcription (using OpenAI Whisper), and AI-powered analysis (sentiment, coaching tips, talk ratio) similar to Gong.
    -   **Activity History:** A central timeline for each lead tracking all interactions (calls, emails, meetings, stage changes).
-   **Collaboration & Productivity:**
    -   **Team Calendar:** A shared internal calendar for the team to view and schedule events/meetings.
    -   **Calendly-like Booking System:** A public-facing booking page for leads to schedule meetings with sales reps, which then appear on the team calendar.
    -   **Task Management:** An Outreach-like system to assign and track sales tasks.
    -   **Team Messaging:** An internal, Slack-like messaging system.
    -   **Content Hub:** A central file repository (e.g., integrated with Google Drive).
-   **AI & Onboarding:**
    -   **AI Copilot/Assistant:** A floating chatbot to guide users on platform usage and tasks.
    -   **AI Sales Coach:** Provide real-time or post-call coaching, sentiment analysis, and performance insights.
-   **UI/UX:** A professional, modern, and highly intuitive turn-key application ready for immediate team deployment. The landing page should reflect its use as an internal tool, not a commercial SaaS product.
